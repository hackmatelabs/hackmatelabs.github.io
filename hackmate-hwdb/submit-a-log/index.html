Submit a Log

  1. Copy TEMPLATE.log and fill it out with your hardware and result.
  2. Pick the two folders it goes in: the feature you used (for example repair-efi-logs/), then your CPU gen inside that (for example intel-gen8/). Intel is intel-genN/, AMD is amd-zenN/ matching your Ryzen chip's Zen architecture.
  3. Name the file after your device, for example thinkpad-t480-i5-8350u.log.
  4. Open a PR, or paste your filled-out log in a hackmate issue or Reddit comment and it'll get added.

It doesn't matter if it worked perfectly or not. Failed and partial results are just as useful as clean ones.
